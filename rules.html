South Texas Precision
Rules

Competition rules

Private-land event in Texas. Date TBA. Bare-bones draft for host review.

Entry & prize

  • Entry: $200 cash per shooter (day-of or as announced).
  • Prize: $5,000 winner-take-all.
  • Roster cap: about 50 (TBA). Host may cancel or postpone for weather or safety; entry returned if the match does not fire.

Eligible rifles

  • Scoped centerfire rifles.
  • Caliber: .223 Remington and up.
  • Safe, legal firearms only.

Course of fire

  • Five-shot groups at 100, 200, and 300 yards.
  • Score = sum of group sizes (inches). Smallest total wins.

Tie breaker

Tied main totals shoot another five-shot group at 300 yards. Smaller shoot-off group wins. If still tied, sudden-death extra five-shot groups at 300 until one shooter is smaller.

Range conduct

  • One shooter on the firing line at a time.
  • No spectators forward of the designated safe area.
  • Follow the range officer. Cease fire means unload, open bolt, wait.
  • Magazines out when not shooting.
  • Eye and ear protection required.

Liability

  • Participants shoot at their own risk on private land.
  • Signed waiver required before any round is fired.
  • Insurance and range details to be confirmed before the match date is set.
